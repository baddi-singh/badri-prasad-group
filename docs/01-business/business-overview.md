Badri Prasad Group is the parent business ecosystem for multiple child companies and products.

## Main Business Goal
Build a trustworthy parent-company website that clearly shows services, group companies, products, careers, and contact channels.

## Primary Objectives
1. Build brand trust.
2. Showcase child companies.
3. Generate business leads.
4. Collect career applications.
5. Maintain professional digital presence.
6. Use the project as a practical QA, DevOps, and software engineering portfolio.
