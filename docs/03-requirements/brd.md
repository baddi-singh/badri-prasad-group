# BRD — Business Requirement Document

## Project Name
Badri Prasad Group Website

## Business Problem
Visitors need a clear, trustworthy, professional website to understand Badri Prasad Group, its child companies, services, careers, and contact options.

## Business Goals
1. Build parent-brand credibility.
2. Show group companies clearly.
3. Capture business inquiries.
4. Capture job applications.
5. Support future products and services.
6. Provide a real-world QA and DevOps portfolio.

## Stakeholders
- Founder / Owner: Badri Prasad
- Visitors / Clients
- Job Applicants
- Future Partners
- Future Developers / QA Engineers

## Success Criteria
- Website works on production domain.
- Contact form saves inquiries.
- Careers form saves applications.
- Admin can manage important data.
- Staging and production environments are separate.
- Documentation and QA process exist.
